How the rotation works

The menu is rewritten every Tuesday after the market run. Dishes leave when their ingredient's moment passes — usually within four weeks. Nothing is frozen over for the sake of the card.

Follow The Lumière Letter in the footer below and the week's changes arrive in your inbox, once, quietly.
