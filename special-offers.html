The Early Bird

Dine between 17:30 and 18:30, Tuesday to Thursday, and take 20% off the entire à la carte. The fire is freshly lit, the room is quiet, and the kitchen has all afternoon to spoil you.

Valid Tue – Thu · 17:30 – 18:30 seatings · until 21 December

Book the early seat

Cellar Sundays

Every Sunday of brunch service, all bottles under $60 are half price at the table. Marc opens something odd from the back shelf each week — ask what it is.

Sundays · 11:00 – 15:00 · dine-in only

Reserve brunch

Birthday Dessert, On Us

Tell us it's a birthday when you book and the pastry section sends out lavender crème brûlée for the whole table, with a candle that behaves itself.

With any main course · mention at booking

Ordering delivery for the first time? Add the code LUMIERE10 at checkout for 10% off, and a starter on the house in every first-order box.

  • Offers cannot be combined with each other or with private-dining menus.
