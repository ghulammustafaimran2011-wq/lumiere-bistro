How it works

From first message to last plate

Step 1

Tell us about the day

Date, guest count, venue and any dietary needs. A short form is all it takes.

Step 2

Receive a proposal

Within two working days you get a menu and a clear per-guest quote — no hidden service charges.

Step 3

Confirm with a deposit

A 25% deposit secures your date. Menus can change free of charge until ten days before the event.

Step 4

We handle the rest

We deliver, set up and — for full-service bookings — serve and clear, so you can enjoy your guests.

Good to know

What's included

Dietary care

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and nut-free options are prepared separately and labelled clearly for your guests.

Seasonal sourcing

We cook from the same local suppliers as the restaurant, so your menu changes with the market — and tastes like it.

Low-waste service

Compostable or reusable serveware as standard, and surplus food goes to local food banks wherever possible.

Questions

Catering FAQ

How far in advance should I book?

Two to three weeks is ideal for drop-off orders, and six weeks or more for full-service dinners — especially in November and December.

Do you deliver outside Alder Bay?

Yes, within 25 km for a small delivery fee quoted up front. Full-service events farther out are considered case by case.

Can I change the guest count after booking?

You can adjust numbers freely until seven days before the event. After that, we can only reduce by up to 10%.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel more than 14 days ahead and your deposit is credited to a future date. Within 14 days, the deposit is retained to cover ingredients and staffing. See our refund policy.
