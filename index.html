Since 2019

A bistro with a long memory and a short menu

LUMIÈRE began as a six-table room behind the fish market. Today the dining room seats forty, but the idea hasn't changed: cook what the season hands us, cook it over fire, and serve it like we've known you for years.

Executive Chef Élise Marchand writes the menu each Tuesday after the market run — which is why the printed card is never quite the same twice.

The details

Three reasons evenings run late here

The Open Fire

Every main passes over embers or under the salamander. Order a seat at the chef's counter and watch the whole thing happen.

The Cellar

Four hundred bins, heavy on the Loire and the Jura. Ask for Marc — the flight he pours off-menu is quietly legendary.

The Hours

We never turn tables twice in an evening. Your table is yours for the night, from apéritif to the last crumb of shortbread.
