Harborfront Quarter

Find Us

48 Lantern Row — the narrow brass-fronted storefront between the old chandlery and the flower stall. If you can smell the woodsmoke, you've gone one door too far.

Getting here

Every route ends at the brass door

By transit

Harborfront station (Lines 2 & 5) is a four-minute walk. Exit toward the fish market, cross Chandler Way, and Lantern Row is the cobbled lane on your right.

By car

Quayside Parking Garage (2-minute walk) offers evening flat rates. Street parking on Lantern Row is scarce — we recommend the garage.

On foot

From the market clock tower, walk seaward along Harborfront Promenade and turn left at the flower stall. Step-free entrance via the brass door ramp.

Make an evening of it

The Harborfront before and after

Before dinner

The fish market runs until 17:00 and the promenade at golden hour is worth the early arrival.

After dinner

A five-minute stroll brings you to the quay lights and the night ferries crossing the bay.

Make a night of it

Several guesthouses sit within the quarter — ask our team when you book and we'll happily point you along.
