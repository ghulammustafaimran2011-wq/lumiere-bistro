How we handle allergies in the kitchen
  • Allergy orders are flagged on the ticket and cooked on a dedicated section of the line.
  • Fryers are shared — we do not claim "free-from" for fried items.
  • For severe allergies, mention it at booking; the chef will plan your courses in advance.
  • Full ingredient lists for any dish are available on request, every service.
