Black Truffle Risotto

Price: 27.00

Carnaroli rice stirred to order with porcini butter and aged parmesan, finished at the pass with shaved black truffle and a thread of chive oil. Our most-ordered main three seasons running.

  • Vegetarian
  • Gluten-free
  • Chef's pick
Ingredients

Carnaroli rice, porcini mushrooms, vegetable stock, porcini butter, aged parmesan (24 months), shallot, white wine, black truffle, chive oil, sea salt.

Allergens

Contains: milk (butter, parmesan), sulphites (white wine). Prepared in a kitchen that handles gluten, nuts, shellfish and egg. Tell your server about any allergy and we will adapt the dish.

Sommelier's pairing

Marc pours a Savagnin from the Jura alongside this dish — nutty, saline, exactly right against the porcini. By the glass or as part of the autumn pairing flight.

Preparation & nutrition

Cooked to order (about 18 minutes). Approximately 640 kcal per serving. Vegetarian rennet used in the parmesan style served here; ask us for the fully plant-based version — it is very good.
