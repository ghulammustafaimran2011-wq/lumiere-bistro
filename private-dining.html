Your evening, our kitchen

Private Dining

Three rooms, one kitchen, and events from eight to thirty guests. Menus are written for the occasion, never templated.

The spaces

Three rooms for three kinds of evening

The Lantern Room

Up to 18 seated · full private bar

Our largest private space, with its own bar and a wall of windows over the promenade. Weddings-adjacent dinners, milestone birthdays, the deal you can't do in an office.

The Cellar Table

Up to 10 guests · one long table

Dinner inside the cellar, surrounded by four hundred bins. Marc hosts, pours, and tells you what he's really saving for himself. Our most requested room.

The Terrace

Up to 24 seated · 30 standing · seasonal

Under the festoon lights, over the harbor air. Heated and covered from October — the shoulder-season dinners out here are a small local legend.

Every private evening includes

  • A dedicated host and your own service team for the night.
  • A menu written with the chef around your occasion, budget and allergies.
  • A wine proposal from the cellar, tasted with you before the date.
  • Screen and AV for presentations, discreetly stowed when not needed.
  • Full dietary handling — vegan, coeliac and allergy guests are planned in from day one.

Minimum spend applies per room; deposits follow our refund policy.
