Our story

A bistro, kept deliberately small

LUMIÈRE is forty seats, one open kitchen, and a promise that nothing on the plate outlives its season.

It started with six tables

In 2019, Élise Marchand left a large hotel kitchen, rented a narrow storefront behind the Harborfront fish market, and opened with six tables, a two-burner stove, and a menu written on butcher paper each morning.

The butcher paper is gone. The habit isn't. Every Tuesday, the menu is rewritten around whatever the market, the dayboats, and the kitchen garden deliver — which is why the dish you loved in June may be replaced by something better in October.

We rebuilt on Lantern Row in 2023, adding the chef's counter, the cellar, and the Lantern Room — but the six original tables still sit by the window, numberless, first-come every service.

What we cook by

Four principles, no exceptions

Season first

The menu follows the market, not the other way round. If it isn't growing here now, it isn't on the card.

Fire and patience

Embers, slow braises, and stocks that start the night before. Fast food exists; this isn't it.

Nothing hidden

An open kitchen means open books — ask any cook what's in your dish and you'll get the full answer.

Everyone eats well

Vegan, gluten-free, nine allergies, or simply fussy — the kitchen adapts without making a scene about it.

The kitchen

Élise Marchand, Executive Chef

Trained in Lyon, tempered by a decade of service lines, Élise cooks the way she talks — plainly, precisely, and with more warmth than she'd admit to. Her tasting menus change with the market; her shortrib recipe has not changed since 2019, and never will.

“A bistro should feel like someone's home — if that someone happened to be an unreasonable perfectionist about stock.”

Milestones

Six years, four rooms, one habit

  1. 2019

    Six tables behind the fish market. The first menu sells out by 19:40 on opening night.

  2. 2021

    The cellar is dug — four hundred bins, heavy on the Loire and the Jura.

  3. 2023

    We move to 48 Lantern Row: chef's counter, Lantern Room, and the original window tables.

  4. 2025

    The kitchen garden on Windrow Farm begins supplying the entire herb program.
